1. **QA-1** [MEDIUM] The Sent head window covers its oldest row's minute even when the list continues → `extension/src/observers/gmail/extract.ts:76`
   - Rule: Hard Rule 5 (UNVERIFIABLE beats a guess); docs/grading.md §3
   - Detail: sentWindow starts the head window at the oldest row's minute: `windowStart: oldest`. Gmail shows times only to the minute. So when another page follows (an enabled 'Older' control), a send in that same minute that is older than the oldest row is listed on page 2. It still falls inside the window, although the pass never saw it. With beforeClaimMin = 2 and afterClaimMin = 45, a claim stamped in minute M+2 has the window [M, M+47). The head fully covers that window, so the claim would grade NOT FOUND although the send happened. docs/adapters.md:473 says the fix is 'open for the sprint close'. The limit is not in docs/attacks-that-work.md.
   - Evidence: QA red test tests/unit/observers/gmail/sent-head-minute.spec.ts: 'AssertionError: expected 1790718900000 to be greater than or equal to 1790718960000'. The window starts at 2026-09-29T17:55:00-04:00, the minute of page 1's oldest row, and page 1 has an enabled Older control.
   - Suggested fix: While the list may continue (page 1 with an enabled Older control, or any page not provably the last), start the head window at the oldest row's minute + 60 s. Keep that minute's rows as evidence. Update the page-1 label's coverage fact and sent-observer.spec.ts:103 (windowStart 17:55 → 17:56). If the closer accepts the limit instead, add a row with a measured rate to docs/attacks-that-work.md and remove the red test.
